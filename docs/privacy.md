# Next Up: Workout Log App privacy policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Next Up is a workout log for iPhone and Apple Watch. The short version: your workouts stay on your devices. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not send your information to the developer or to anyone else.

## What the app stores

The workouts you log (exercises, sets, weights, reps and dates), your gyms and their equipment, the exercises and machines you add, and your settings (such as your weekly goal and rest timer). All of this is saved only on your iPhone, and is shared with your own Apple Watch so you can log from your wrist. It is removed if you delete the app.

## Apple Health

If you choose to connect Apple Health, Next Up reads your runs, walks and body weight to show them next to your lifting, reads heart rate and calories on Apple Watch while you lift, and saves each lifting session to Apple Health as a strength workout. You can turn any of this off at any time in the Health app or in iPhone Settings.

Information from Apple Health is used only to show it to you inside the app. It is never sent to the developer, never shared with or sold to anyone, and never used for advertising or marketing.

## Exporting your data

Settings has an option to export your workouts as a spreadsheet file. The file is created only when you ask for it, and goes only where you choose to send or save it.

## Children

Next Up is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
